WHY WE OPENED OUR DOORS

Good food
brings us together.

Green Market started with a question: what if your weekly shop felt less like a chore and more like a small, lovely part of your day?

So we built a market around the things we care about: fresh produce, honest essentials, local makers and a warm hello at the door. Nothing complicated. Just good things, chosen thoughtfully.

With care, the Green Market team ✳
